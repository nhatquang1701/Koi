# Koi v1.2.0

Released 2026-11-21. **Koi v1.2.0** delivers a major engine-core, evaluation,
multi-threading, protocol, and packaging upgrade over v1.1.6—crossing the **2600
Elo** milestone and reaching **2727 Elo** (95% CI **2674–2778**) against
Stockfish 19 `UCI_Elo` anchors.

## Highlights

- **Crosses 2600 Elo (+998 Elo over v1.1.5) to 2727 Elo.** Measured at 200 ms/move
  with 4 threads, `Hash=512`, `no-book`, and the promoted h128 NNUE network
  (`koi.nnue`), Koi v1.2.0 improves from `1729 Elo` in v1.1.5 to **`2727 Elo`**
  (95% CI **`2674–2778`**), scoring **`67.19%`** against Stockfish 19 `UCI_Elo 2600`
  and **`17.19%`** against `UCI_Elo 3000`.
- **Dual HalfKA v4, Finny tables, and fused SIMD/NEON inference (`15576f8f`, `78740090`, `f94fc0b2`, `b01b6197`).**
  Upgraded the NNUE accumulator path with Dual HalfKA v4, Finny king-bucket
  caching, lazy accumulator materialization, hoisted deltas, fused AVX2/NEON v4
  inference, and hot-path CPU optimizations (`+53%` MNPS in `f94fc0b2`, plus
  an additional `+7.9%` faster `bench_8fen` time-to-depth and `+17.8 Elo` in
  `1+0` clock self-play in `b01b6197`).
- **Fancy Magic Bitboards and O(1) pin-legal move generation (`037ef8ac`, `8f49fce9`).**
  Replaced slow board scans with Fancy Magic Bitboards, O(1) pin legality checks,
  and streamed tactical/check move generation, plus lockless transposition tables
  with improved Lazy SMP scaling (`4e66418a`) and expanded minor/major/king static
  eval correction history (`0b5c0d3f`).
- **Multi-protocol support and GUI tooling (`3d00326c`, `5f767649`).**
  Added `Protocol` option / `--protocol` support for **UCI**, **XBoard/CECP v2**,
  **USI**, **UCCI**, and **UCI_Cyclone** presets on standard 8x8 chess, along with
  `--version`, `--engine-json`, and sound PGN move annotation (`tools/measurement/annotate_pgn.py`).
- **Compact Stockfish-verified CC0 opening book (`a1796fd2`).**
  Bundled a 522,320-byte Stockfish-verified CC0 Polyglot `book.bin` flat beside
  the engine executables in release archives.
- **GPLv3 license and author attribution (`eebb0798`, `138572f6`, `b01b6197`).**
  Switched the project license from MIT to **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`) and
  updated Windows PE `LegalCopyright` (`Copyright (C) 2026 Nguyễn Nhật Quang (GPL-3.0-or-later)`)
  and UCI `id author` (`Nguyễn Nhật Quang`).

## Compatibility and upgrade notes

- **GPLv3 license:** Koi is now licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`), and Windows PE metadata / UCI identity report author `Nguyễn Nhật Quang`.
- **UCI identity:** Handshake prints `id name Koi 1.2.0` and `id author Nguyễn Nhật Quang`. Use `koi-engine --engine-json` for GUI metadata import in Pawn Appétit / En Croissant or `koi-engine --version` for CLI inspection.
- **Minimal self-contained archives:** Each platform archive includes strictly the engine binaries (`koi-engine`, plus `koi-engine-avx2`/`koi-engine-avx512` on x86-64), flat `koi.nnue`, flat `book.bin` (Stockfish-verified CC0 book), and `LICENSE`.
- **Optional tablebases:** Syzygy `.rtbw`/`.rtbz` files are auto-detected from `syzygy/` beside the executable when `SyzygyPath` is unset, and are never bundled inside archives.

## Known limitations

- `UCI_LimitStrength` and `UCI_Elo` are not implemented for Koi (Stockfish 19's
  `UCI_Elo` is used purely as an external rating reference).
- Syzygy tablebase data is not packaged.
